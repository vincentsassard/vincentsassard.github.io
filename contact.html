My Research Group

Computational Seismology & Tectonics

I am a member of the Computational Seismology and Tectonics group at Purdue University, led by Dr. Xiaotao Yang. Our group uses seismic imaging, numerical modeling, and large-scale computing to understand the structure and dynamics of the Earth's lithosphere and mantle.

I am open to discussing potential collaborations, my research, or opportunities in seismology and geophysics. The best way to reach me is by email.
